Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers regarding consultations, tests, and door-to-balloon times.

Door-to-Balloon time measures the exact duration from when a heart attack patient enters the hospital door to when the blocked coronary artery is reopened using a balloon catheter. While global guidelines recommend under 90 minutes, Dr. Sanjay achieved this in 16 minutes, recognized in the Limca Book of Records for preserving maximum viable heart muscle.

In transradial angioplasty, instruments enter through the radial artery in your wrist instead of the femoral artery in the groin. This drastically lowers bleeding complications, eliminates prolonged bed immobility, and enables patient's to sit up, walk, and eat within 2 to 4 hours of the procedure.
